enlace_auth.signup#

Self-service sign-up and email-code sign-in.

make_signup_router mounts /auth/email/* (a one-time code mailed to the address, verify-before-create), the verify-first /auth/register, and self-service deletion (/auth/account/delete). The pieces are seams the plugin wires from [auth.signup]: SignupPolicy (who may create an account from which app), a rate limiter (enlace_auth.ratelimit), and a bot check (TimedHoneypot). The design and its research: misc/docs/research/self-signup-synthesis.md.
